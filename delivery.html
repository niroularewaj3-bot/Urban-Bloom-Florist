Good to know

Delivery & FAQs

We deliver flowers around Auckland and make each order fresh. Here are the details to help you plan your delivery.

01 / Area

Delivery area

We deliver across central Auckland and nearby suburbs. Please contact us to confirm delivery to your address, especially for locations further out.

02 / Fee

Delivery fee

Local delivery starts at $12. The exact fee depends on the delivery address and will be confirmed when you enquire.

03 / Timing

Timing

Same-day delivery may be available for orders placed before 11:00 am, subject to flower and driver availability. For a specific date, please order ahead.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a particular flower or colour?

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind and we will let you know what is in season. We cannot guarantee every variety, but we can suggest a suitable alternative.

Can I include a message with my flowers?

Yes. We can include a handwritten card. Please provide your message when you get in touch.

What happens if the recipient is not home?

Please include a phone number and any safe-place or access instructions. We will contact you if a delivery cannot be completed safely.

Can I arrange delivery for a wedding or business?

Yes. Wedding, event and regular business deliveries are planned individually. Visit our Weddings & Events or Corporate page for more details.

Are the flowers exactly as pictured?

Our arrangements are made with fresh seasonal flowers, so varieties can change. We aim to keep the overall colour palette and value consistent.
