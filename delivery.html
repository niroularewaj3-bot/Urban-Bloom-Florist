Delivery & FAQs

I make every order fresh and deliver across Auckland, so your flowers arrive ready to share. Here are a few details to make planning easy.

01 / Area

Where I deliver

I deliver across central Auckland and nearby suburbs. If you’re further out, get in touch and I’ll check whether I can bring your flowers to you.

02 / Fee

A simple delivery fee

Local delivery starts at $12. The exact fee depends on the address, and I’ll confirm it with you before your order is finalised.

03 / Timing

When to order

Same-day delivery may be possible for orders placed before 11:00 am, depending on the flowers and driver availability. For a date close to your heart, ordering ahead is best.

A few things you might be wondering

Good to know

Can you use a particular flower or colour?

Tell me what you love and I’ll check what’s in season. I can’t promise every variety will be available, but I’ll do my best to find a lovely alternative.

Can I include a personal message?

Of course. I’d be happy to add a handwritten card. Just send me your words when you get in touch.

What if no one is home?

Please share a phone number and any safe-place or access instructions. If I can’t leave the flowers safely, I’ll contact you to work out what to do next.

Can you deliver for a wedding or business?

Yes. I plan wedding, event and regular business deliveries personally. Take a look at my Weddings & Events or Corporate page to explore the possibilities.

Will my flowers look exactly like the pictures?

Flowers change with the seasons, so each arrangement is a little unique. I’ll keep the colours and feeling you chose, and make sure the flowers are fresh and full of life.
