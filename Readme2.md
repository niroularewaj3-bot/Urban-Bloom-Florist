# Urban Bloom Florist — Version 2

## About the website

Urban Bloom Florist is a responsive, multi-page website for Rewaj's Auckland
floristry business. Its personal story connects Rewaj's roots in Itahari, Nepal
with a love of flowers, nature and thoughtful arrangements.

Version 2 keeps the original florist content and introduces a new visual theme
and a redesigned navigation layout.

## Version 2 updates

- **Cool-blue colour theme:** soft blue page backgrounds, pale blue section
  surfaces, deep navy headings and navigation, and brighter blue accents for
  links and primary buttons.
- **Left-side desktop navigation:** the header becomes a fixed, full-height
  vertical rail with the Urban Bloom Florist logo and page links.
- **Prominent flower link:** “Shop flowers” is styled as the navigation's
  primary call to action.
- **Responsive navigation:** at tablet and mobile widths, the side rail changes
  to a compact top header with a menu button. The Services dropdown remains
  available in the expanded menu.
- **Updated shared styling:** the blue palette is applied to page backgrounds,
  text, buttons, forms, borders, section highlights, and the footer.

## Pages

- `index.html` — introduction to Rewaj, featured bouquets and service links.
- `flowers.html` — seasonal arrangements, bouquet information and occasions.
- `weddings.html` — personal flowers, ceremony arrangements and reception
  styling.
- `corporate.html` — workplace flowers, business events and client or staff
  gifts.
- `delivery.html` — delivery areas, fees, timing and frequently asked questions.
- `contact.html` — contact details and a demonstration enquiry form.

## Other features

- Mobile navigation and a Services dropdown, with expanded state announced
  through accessible button attributes.
- A keyboard-accessible “Skip to content” link and clear focus indicators.
- Local photographs and locally hosted SVG bouquet illustrations.
- Seasonal bouquet descriptions and indicative prices.
- A client-side contact form demonstration. It does not send or store enquiries;
  customers are directed to call or email instead.

## Project files

- `css/style.css` — shared layout, responsive breakpoints and Version 2 theme.
- `js/script.js` — mobile menu, Services dropdown and demonstration form
  behavior.
- `images/` — locally hosted SVG illustrations.
- `image 1.jpeg` and `image 2.jpeg` — project photographs used on the pages.

## View the website

Open `index.html` in a browser. The site uses local HTML, CSS, JavaScript and
images and does not require a build step.
