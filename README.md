# Urban Bloom Florist

## About this project

This website is part of my ICL College project work. I have implemented a
responsive, multi-page florist website with bouquet listings, wedding and
corporate flower services, delivery information, and a contact form
demonstration. I have also added navigation for mobile screens and original,
locally hosted SVG flower illustrations.

I am trying to give the website a more personal look and feel. The writing
shares my story of moving from Itahari, Nepal to New Zealand and growing up
surrounded by flora and fauna. I want the site to feel warm and welcoming, and
to show the care and meaning behind each bouquet.
